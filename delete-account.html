Account deletion

Delete Your ComicCluck Account

This page explains how to ask for your ComicCluck account and data to be deleted, what we delete, and what we keep.

Applies to: ComicCluck web app and Android app Last Updated: October 5, 2026
Step by step

How to Request Deletion

  1. Email domesticdog5678@gmail.com with the subject ComicCluck account deletion request.
  2. In the email, include your ComicCluck handle and the email address you sign in with (your Google or Discord email). This lets us confirm the account is yours.
  3. We will delete your account and its data within 30 days and email you when it is done.

Send the request from the same email address you use to sign in to ComicCluck. If you send it from a different address, we may ask you to confirm ownership before we delete anything.

If you only want to remove some of your content, you can delete individual comics, drafts, sprites, and other uploads yourself inside the app, without deleting your account.

What we delete

Data That Is Deleted

When we delete your account, we permanently delete the data tied to it:

  • Your account, handle, display name, bio, profile picture, banner, social links, and settings.
  • Your email address and sign-in connection to Google or Discord.
  • Your comics, frames, drafts, stories, and ToonScroll settings.
  • Your uploaded sprites, sprite packs, backgrounds, effects, audio, and images.
  • Your comments, replies, reactions, and ratings.
  • Your follows, favorites, playlists, and collaborator and co-owner links.
  • Your online status, blocks, and messages you sent in direct and team messages.

This is a deletion, not a deactivation. We do not just hide or freeze the account.
