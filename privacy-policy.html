Privacy Policy

This policy explains what data ComicCluck collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

Effective: October 5, 2026   Last Updated: October 5, 2026

Section 1

Overview

This policy covers the ComicCluck web app at zaz444.github.io/ComicCluck and the ComicCluck Android app, which displays that same web app. Some pages of the site still show the earlier name ComicCore. It is the same service, and this policy applies to it.

ComicCluck is a comic creation and sharing platform run by an independent developer (creator handle: jeffyplays). For any privacy question or request, email domesticdog5678@gmail.com.

You sign in with Google or Discord. Your comics, profile, and comments are public unless a setting says otherwise. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party advertising or analytics tools.

Section 2

What We Collect

Account and sign-in

When you sign in with Google or Discord, that provider gives us your email address and basic profile details such as your name and avatar. For Discord, the sign-in request also asks for permission to see the list of servers you belong to. We never receive your Google or Discord password.

Profile information you add

  • Handle, display name, bio, profile picture, and banner.
  • Social links you choose to add, such as Instagram, TikTok, or an email address.
  • Settings and privacy choices, such as who can see your followers or team.
  • Your online status, if you set one.

Content you create or upload

  • Comics, frames, drafts, stories, and ToonScroll settings.
  • Sprites, sprite packs, backgrounds, effects, audio, and images you upload or add to galleries.
  • Comments, replies, reactions, ratings, and mentions.

Social activity and messages

  • Follows, collaborator and co-owner lists, playlists, and favorites.
  • Squad posts, squad chat messages, and invites.
  • Messages and reactions sent through the platform, and team or support tickets and requests you submit.
  • Reports you file about other users or content, and blocks you set.

Collected automatically

  • IP address. We record your IP address when you log in. We use it for abuse prevention and to enforce bans.
  • View counts. We count how many times each comic is viewed.
  • Technical data. Like any website, the servers that host and deliver ComicCluck can see standard request information, such as browser type and the pages requested.

Stored on your device

ComicCluck saves data in your browser or app using local storage, session storage, IndexedDB, and a service worker cache. This holds your sign-in session, theme and editor preferences, unsaved drafts, and cached comics, sprites, and images so the app works offline. We do not use this storage to track you across other sites. Clearing your browser or app data removes it.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, contacts, microphone or camera data, or payment information. The Android app does not request device permissions for these.

Section 3

How We Use Your Data

  • Sign you in and run your account.
  • Save, publish, display, and deliver your comics and other content.
  • Power social features such as follows, comments, squads, and messages.
  • Moderate content, handle reports, enforce our rules, and stop banned users from returning.
  • Fix bugs, keep the service secure, and send service-related notices.
  • Respond when you contact us.

We do not use your content to train AI models, and we do not use your data for advertising.

Section 4

Who Can See and Receive Your Data

Other users

  • Published comics, your public profile, comments, and approved gallery submissions can be seen by anyone who uses ComicCluck.
  • Squad content is visible to squad members.
  • Your email address is not shown publicly. Only the owner and moderators can see it. If you add an email to your social links, that link is public.

Legal and safety

We may disclose data if the law requires it, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect the safety of users and the service. If ComicCluck is ever transferred to someone else, your data may move with it, and we will tell you first.

Section 5

How Long We Keep Data

  • Account, profile, and content data are kept while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile, comics, drafts, uploads, and other data tied to it, as described below.
  • We may keep a limited record for security and abuse prevention, such as banned IP addresses and moderation logs, when needed to stop repeat abuse or follow the law.
  • Messages you sent in squad chats may remain visible to other members with your name removed, so conversations still make sense.
  • Backups may hold deleted data for a short time before they are overwritten.

Section 6

Deleting Your Account

You can ask us to delete your account and the data tied to it at any time. We do not just deactivate the account. We remove the data.

  1. Email domesticdog5678@gmail.com with the subject "ComicCluck account deletion request".
  2. Include your ComicCluck handle and the email address you signed in with, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. We will delete your account and associated data within 30 days and confirm by email.

You can also delete individual comics, drafts, sprites, and other content yourself from inside the app at any time, without deleting your account.

The full details, including what is deleted and what is kept, are on our Delete Account page.

Section 7

Your Choices and Rights

You can edit your profile, change your privacy settings, delete your content, and sign out at any time. You can also email us to:

Access

Get a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Correction

Fix information that is wrong.

Deletion

Have your account and data deleted.

Objection

Object to or limit certain uses of your data.

If you live in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, California, or another place with privacy laws, you may have additional rights under those laws, and we will honor them. Where the law requires a legal basis for processing, ours are providing the service you asked for, our legitimate interest in keeping the platform safe, and your consent where we ask for it. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account or contacting us. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Section 8

Security

Data moves between your device and our servers over HTTPS. Access to stored data is limited by database access rules and account permissions. No online service can promise perfect security, so please keep your Google and Discord accounts secure.

Section 9

Children

ComicCluck is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it. If you are under the age of digital consent where you live, you need a parent or guardian's permission to use ComicCluck.

Section 10

Where Data Is Processed

Our service providers may store and process data in the United States and other countries. By using ComicCluck, you understand your data may be transferred there.

Section 11

Changes to This Policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes, we will also post a notice in the app.
